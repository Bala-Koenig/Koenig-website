Frequently asked questions

What is AWS Skill Builder?

AWS Skill Builder is AWS's digital learning platform, with 900+ courses, hands-on labs, game-based learning, immersive solution-building, and official certification exam prep. Koenig helps you choose the right subscription and supports your team through the learning journey.

What's included in the free tier?

AWS offers a free tier directly, with limited access to digital courses, learning plans, and digital badges. Subscribing through Koenig (Annual or Team) unlocks the full course catalog, labs, game-based learning, exam prep, and more — see the comparison table above for the full breakdown.

What's the difference between the Annual and Team subscriptions?

Annual ($449/year) gives one learner full access to all digital courses, game-based learning, labs, immersive solution-building, exam prep content, and AWS Digital Classroom courses. Team ($449/seat/year, 5-seat minimum) includes everything in Annual plus tools and team training to build cloud skills at scale — admin dashboard, reporting, single sign-on, and group-based learning.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Annual and Team subscriptions are annual commitments — talk to Koenig about the cancellation and renewal terms that apply to you or your organization.

What languages is this available in?

Subscriptions are available in multiple languages, including English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, and both Latin American and European Spanish, across 200+ countries and territories.

What certifications does the exam prep cover?

Official exam prep content — Exam Prep Review, Exam Prep Practice, the AWS Escape Room: Exam Prep, and full-length AWS Certification Official Pretests and Practice Exams — is included across the AWS certification portfolio, from Cloud Practitioner through Associate, Professional, and Specialty levels.
