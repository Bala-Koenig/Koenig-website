Frequently asked questions

What is the Red Hat Learning Subscription?

A one-year subscription giving access to Red Hat's catalog of technical training courses, available through Koenig in two tiers — Standard and Premium. It covers everything from Red Hat Enterprise Linux basics through advanced cloud, automation, containerization, and AI topics.

What's the difference between Standard and Premium?

Standard gives you the full self-paced training catalog, 400 hours of cloud-based hands-on labs per user, exam readiness tools, and 5 certification exams plus 5 retakes. Premium includes everything in Standard, plus live, virtual instructor-led classes with certified Red Hat experts — up to 2 per course.

When does my subscription start?

You can choose a start date within 90 days of purchase, noted on your order. If no date is chosen, the subscription starts on the order date.

How often are live courses offered?

Each live, virtual, instructor-led course (Premium only) runs once every 12 months in English.

Can my team or organization get a subscription?

Yes — Koenig can help set up Red Hat Learning Subscriptions for teams and enterprises, including bulk and multi-seat orders. Contact us to discuss your team's needs.
