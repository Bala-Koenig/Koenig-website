Frequently asked questions

What is SAP Learning Hub?

The redesigned SAP Learning Hub is a digital learning solution that supports learners in building and maintaining SAP software skills. It provides guided, expert-led and hands-on learning opportunities to enable upskilling across the SAP solution portfolio. Learners also get access to all the enablement they need to become and stay certified with four certification exam attempts every year included in the subscription.

What does a subscription to SAP Learning Hub include?

A subscription to SAP Learning Hub provides access to a vast number of guided learning resources and management capabilities, such as:

  • Expert-led live sessions covering deep dives and preparations to stay certified
  • Access to hands-on practice systems and sandboxed SAP environments, pre-filled with data and exercises to experiment with solutions
  • Four SAP Certification exam attempts every year and access to enablement and assessment to stay certified
  • Learning analytics capabilities to support individual and team management
What learning formats are available?

With access to both self-paced and guided learning resources, learners can follow learning journeys tailored for specific roles and skill levels to upskill in SAP solutions and topics in a self-paced way. Subscribers can also access guided learning resources to deepen their knowledge, such as expert-led live sessions and hands-on practice opportunities.

How can learners validate their skills?

For foundation-level skills, learners can gain a Record of Achievement by successfully completing quizzes in dedicated learning journeys. For intermediate and advanced skills, the SAP Certification programme offers trusted recognition and validation of expertise through certification exams. With a subscription to SAP Learning Hub, learners can attempt up to four SAP Certification exams every year.

What languages are available?

The content library is available in up to nine languages, including English, French, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Russian. Please note not all content is available in all languages listed.

What do you need to get and stay certified?

Learners can get and stay certified with one single subscription to the enhanced SAP Learning Hub. A subscription provides you access to all resources you need to prepare for certifications, take the certification exams with four attempts included and stay certified with access to yearly assessments.

In addition, you can also purchase one or six SAP Certification exam attempts. To stay certified, you have a simple path with a subscription to SAP Learning Hub, with access to short and easy yearly assessments.

Alternatively, if you don't have an SAP Learning Hub subscription, you still have the option to purchase and retake an SAP Certification exam every year.

What is the difference between taking a certification exam and completing assessments to stay certified?

An SAP Certification exam is a comprehensive valuation of knowledge and skills required to earn a certification in a specific SAP solution or role. The exams are proctored and can typically take up to three hours. Candidates are required to answer 40-80 questions, out of which 60-70% correctly to pass the exam. If candidates fail the exam, they need to repurchase an SAP Certification exam attempt and retake the exam.

In contrast to SAP Certification exams, stay certified assessments are short, not proctored quizzes with unlimited trials. The assessment covers only the knowledge needed between releases to make sure learners stay continuously up to date with the solution updates. Additionally with the SAP Learning Hub subscription, learners have access to dedicated learning materials, like live sessions to prepare for the assessments. Stay certified assessments are accessible only with an SAP Learning Hub subscription.

If a learner doesn't have an SAP Learning Hub subscription to complete the stay certified assessment and their SAP Certification expires, they need to repurchase and retake the full SAP Certification exam.
